Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are

RackTimer (Android app fr.tactapp.racktimer) is published by TactApp, an individual developer. Contact: tactapp.apps@gmail.com.

Data we collect

None. RackTimer does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings (player names, colours, timer duration, language), scoresheets, game history and turn statistics are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Voice features

Spoken announcements use your phone's text-to-speech engine. When you tap “Dictate”, the app asks your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google) to transcribe what you say; RackTimer itself does not record or keep audio and does not request the microphone permission. That service is governed by its provider's privacy policy.

Sharing

A scoresheet image is shared only when you tap “Share”, through the Android share menu, to the app you choose.

Permissions

Vibration only (vibrate at the end of turns).

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
